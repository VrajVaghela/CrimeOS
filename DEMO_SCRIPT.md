# Crime OS AI — 5-Minute Live Demo Script
This walkthrough guides hackathon judges and evaluators through the complete golden-path flow of the **Crime OS AI** prototype.

---

## 👥 Demo Personas
* **Inspector Asha Patel (Investigating Officer - `IO`)**: Conducts the main intake, investigation steps, evidence uploads, and dispatches.
* **SHO Vikram Singh (Station House Officer - `SHO`)**: Approves legal request drafts and case synchronizations.
* **Legal Advisor Meera Shah (Legal Auditor - `LEGAL`)**: Verifies AI-suggested statutory citations under the BNS/BNSS/BSA.

---

## 🚶‍♂️ Step-by-Step Walkthrough

### 🎬 Part 1: Reset the Demo Slate
Start with a clean database. Run this script in the terminal:
```bash
cd backend
python -m app.seeds.run
```
This drops the tables, regenerates schema migrations, seeds standard SOP/BNS datasets, and establishes two empty demo cases in the registry.

---

### 📥 Part 2: Multimodal Ingestion & Ingestion Review (`IO` role)
1. **Login**: Go to the login page and authenticate as **Investigating Officer**:
   * **Username**: `io`
   * **Password**: `demo123`
2. **Dashboard**: Note the active cases listed in the registry. Click on the first case: **"ERH26-CYB-0001: UPI fraud complaint awaiting ingestion"**.
3. **Ingestion Upload**:
   * Under the **Ingestion** tab, drag and drop the mock complaint file (e.g. a Gujarati complaint letter, Hindi audio file, or a handwritten image).
   * Click **Process Complaint**. The page shows a loading skeleton while calling Gemini Flash.
4. **Side-by-Side Review**:
   * Review the original text next to the English translation.
   * View the extracted entities card. Note the amber warning rings around low-confidence entities (e.g. misspelled phone numbers or suspect handles).
   * Edit one of the entity fields to show human-in-the-loop review, and click **Confirm Entities**.

---

### 🗺️ Part 3: Investigation Path & Citations (`IO` role)
1. **SOP Path Stepper**: Click the **Investigation** tab.
   * Review the vertical step timeline auto-generated by the RAG model mapping the complaint details to the *Cyber Financial Fraud SOP*.
   * Click **SOP Grounding** on any step to open a modal dialog displaying exact citations and excerpts from the grounded policy document.
   * Select a step status dropdown and change it from `PENDING` to `IN PROGRESS` to update the case log.

---

### ✉️ Part 4: Legal Requests Approval Loop (`IO` ➡️ `SHO` ➡️ `IO`)
1. **Generate Draft (`IO`)**:
   * Locate the step for bank account containment. Click the **Generate BANK_FREEZE Request** button.
   * The page redirects to the **Requests** tab with a filled Jinja2 template addressed to the target nodal bank.
   * Modify the email recipient or name inside the letter body, then click **Submit for Approval**.
   * The request status updates to `DRAFT` and displays `"Awaiting SHO Approval"`.
2. **Approve Request (`SHO`)**:
   * Log out of the `io` account, and log in as the Station House Officer:
     * **Username**: `sho`
     * **Password**: `demo123`
   * On the **SHO Dashboard**, view the **Legal Requests Approval Queue**.
   * Locate the pending draft, review it, and click **Approve**. The status shifts to `APPROVED`.
3. **Dispatch & Simulate (`IO`)**:
   * Log out and log back in as `io` (`demo123`). Return to the case's **Requests** tab.
   * The button now reads **Dispatch Request**. Click it. This dispatches an email to the bank (using SMTP settings).
   * Click **Simulate Response** to mock the bank nodal officer's transaction logs and KYC reply.
   * Go to the **Responses** tab to inspect the parsed bank transaction metadata and AI insight table.

---

### 📷 Part 5: Evidence AI Tagging Gallery (`IO` role)
1. **Upload Evidence**: Click the **Evidence** tab.
   * Click **Upload Image Evidence** and select a screenshot (e.g., WhatsApp chat threats, a fake website portal, or transaction receipt).
   * The loader shows "Analyzing Image..." while Gemini Vision reads the file.
2. **AI Tagging Review**:
   * View the newly added gallery card.
   * Observe the AI-generated forensic description, auto-extracted tag badges, confidence score rating, and bulleted list of flagged elements (e.g., suspicious IP address or compromised account names).

---

### ⚖️ Part 6: Legal Citations Audit (`LEGAL` role)
1. **Login as Legal**: Log out and log in as the Legal Advisor:
   * **Username**: `legal`
   * **Password**: `demo123`
2. **Dashboard**: Note the cases listed under the **Legal Section Audit** list. Open **ERH26-CYB-0001**.
3. **Audit suggested sections**:
   * Go to the **Investigation** tab.
   * Look at the **Legal Grounding** sidebar on the right.
   * Review the AI-suggested BNS/BNSS/BSA citations and reasoning.
   * Click **Verify Citation** to approve it, or **Flag Inapplicable** to suggest revisions. The status updates in real-time.

---

### 🌐 Part 7: eGujcop / CCTNS Synchronization (`IO` or `SHO`)
1. **Sync Action**: Log back in as `io` or `sho`.
2. **Sync Dialog**: Click the prominent **Sync to CCTNS** button in the case header.
   * Inspect the dialog preview showing the complete JSON payload compiled from complaint data, verified legal citations, and suspect entities.
3. **Push confirmation**: Click **Confirm Sync & Push**.
   * The button changes to a success badge: **CCTNS Synced** with a glowing green checkmark.
   * The case status updates, and a national FIR Number is allocated.

---

### 📝 Part 8: Case Summary & Case Logs
1. **Case Summary**: Under the **Summary** tab, view the markdown-rendered case summary. Click **Regenerate Summary** to incorporate the newly added evidence files and CCTNS sync logs.
2. **Audit Trails**: Go to the **Audit** tab to verify that every ingestion, status change, request approval, evidence upload, and CCTNS synchronization has been logged in the append-only tamper-proof timeline.
